# 原创图像记录

以下两项由内置 imagegen 生成，每项一次，未调用 API/CLI 后备方案。网站文件为 `assets/vinyl-crimson.png` 和 `assets/cloud-transition.png`。

## 黑胶唱片

Use case: product-mockup
Asset type: a single original vinyl-record bitmap asset for a premium NetEase music concept landing page, to rotate in code.
Primary request: one photorealistic black vinyl record, viewed perfectly straight down, with a completely blank rich-crimson circular center label and a tiny central spindle hole.
Scene/backdrop: genuinely transparent background with an alpha channel; isolated object, no ground plane.
Composition/framing: 1:1 square image around 1024 by 1024 pixels. The full record is centered exactly, a geometrically perfect frontal circle, with modest even transparent padding on all sides. Entire circumference is visible. Orthographic top-down perspective, no tilt or foreshortening.
Lighting/mood: premium studio product photography, subtle elegant silver reflections that reveal the fine concentric grooves without washing out the black vinyl.
Color palette: near-black vinyl, rich deep-crimson label, restrained silver highlights.
Materials/textures: intricate glossy concentric groove texture, authentic polished vinyl and matte paper label.
Constraints: exactly one record; no text, letters, numbers, logos, watermark, sleeve, turntable, tonearm, extra objects, cast shadow outside the disc, or decorative frame. No checkerboard baked into the image. True transparency outside the record. Do not crop the circle.

## 烟云过渡

Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: original panoramic cloud transition bitmap for a music landing page.
Primary request: a cinematic dense charcoal-black smoke and cloud bank isolated on a genuinely transparent background. Wide horizontal panoramic composition, approximately 3:1 aspect ratio. This spans the full screen width between a black hero above and a red section below.
Composition: the upper edge of the image is filled continuously across the entire width with dense opaque nearly black cloud, flush to the top and both lateral edges. The cloud descends unevenly, forming an organically scalloped lower edge with intricate soft billows and delicate wispy tendrils that taper into true transparent alpha in the lower third. Substantial cloud mass in the top two thirds, transparent area beneath. Clouds fill left to right without isolated floating clumps.
Style: photorealistic atmospheric smoke, cinematic volumetric depth, subtly lit charcoal and near-black layered billows, dramatic yet restrained. Rich detail readable against dark black and crimson webpage sections.
Constraints: cloud and smoke only. No text, logo, watermark, UI, scene, sky, scenery, ground plane, horizon, people, objects, lightning, bright glow, color tint, decorative border, or baked checkerboard. Actual transparent alpha background underneath and around the organically wispy lower edge. Do not make a rectangular opaque background below the clouds.
